# Public review scope

Maintenance decision of 20 October 2026.

This public working package covers the general CONT notes, the controlled local HTTP diagnostic and the finite prime-continuation experiments listed in README.md. General mathematical statements retain their explicit hypotheses and classical-literature boundaries. This is not a stable release or a priority claim.

Private-source examples and links are outside this package. Historical editorial exclusions do not authorize adding excluded material. Every future addition requires a review of the exact files, sources, rights and disclosure scope. A calendar date alone does not approve publication.

This change replaces unnecessary references to excluded projects in the current entry points. It introduces no new permission, disclosure or claim of a comprehensive confidentiality audit. Git history remains preserved.
